Privacy Policy

FlashAI · Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how FlashAI collects, uses and protects information when you use the app. Reading this policy does not mean you allow data to be sent to OpenAI; AI features require your separate consent.

1. Who is responsible

This policy applies to the FlashAI app, operated by Truong Huy in Vietnam.

2. Information we collect

When you create an account or sign in, FlashAI only uses the profile information needed to authenticate you and display your account:

  • Profile picture (avatar).
  • Account name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account ID used to sync and protect your data.

3. Signing in with third-party services

You can sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook or email through Supabase Auth. The sign-in provider handles your credentials under its own policy. FlashAI receives the profile information needed to create, maintain and display your account.

4. Data you create in the app

FlashAI stores decks, flashcards, images, audio, study progress, review schedules, study streaks and reminder preferences to provide the features you use. If you send feedback, its content, along with your name and email, may be stored to handle your request.

If you turn on study reminders, FlashAI stores your device’s notification token, linked to your account and platform, to send reminders. When you turn reminders off or sign out, the token on the device is disabled for notifications. The app language you choose is also stored in your profile so that reminders and other server messages are sent in that language.

FlashAI only sends data to OpenAI after you give separate consent in the AI Permission dialog. Camera or photo library permission, and reading this policy, do not replace consent to send data to OpenAI.

4a. Sharing data with OpenAI for AI features

FlashAI uses OpenAI to provide AI features and Microsoft Azure AI Speech to score pronunciation. Data is sent through FlashAI’s server on Supabase to these providers for processing.

  • Images you pick, take or share into image analysis, including any text and information in them, to recognize their content and create flashcards.
  • Text, vocabulary, topics, flashcard content and imported file content that you use with AI, to create or complete flashcards.
  • Descriptions, vocabulary, examples and deck topics used to create illustrations or cover images.
  • Text to be read aloud by an AI voice.
  • In Pronunciation mode: a short recording of your voice reading the word (up to 6 seconds) is sent, together with that word, to Microsoft Azure AI Speech to score your pronunciation. FlashAI does not store the recording and only uses the microphone when you press the record button.

4b. Choosing and withdrawing AI permission

Content may contain personal information. Only send content you have the right to use. You can decline and keep studying or create flashcards manually.

Your choice applies to AI features on this account and device. You can withdraw it in Profile → AI Permission. Withdrawing stops new AI requests; data already sent is handled under the Privacy Policy.

If you decline, FlashAI does not send new requests to OpenAI and does not hold or deduct AI Credits for blocked requests. You can still study saved content and create flashcards manually. Reinstalling the app, signing in to another account or changing what is shared may ask for your consent again.

FlashAI does not attach your name, email, password or sign-in token to requests sent to OpenAI. However, the images, text and content you provide may contain personal information, so check the content before using AI.

4c. Referral codes, partner links and commission payouts

When you open a FlashAI partner’s referral link (such as flashai.academy/r/…), FlashAI records the visit: the referral code, device type (iOS, Android or computer), country and a one-way hash made from the IP address and browser for that day, to count visitors. FlashAI does not store IP addresses.

When you enter a referral code, or install the app from Google Play through a referral link, FlashAI links that code to your account to give you AI Credits and to calculate the partner’s commission when you buy a paid plan. Partners only see aggregate figures and an anonymous customer ID; FlashAI does not share your name, email or study content with partners.

Every account is also a referral partner: FlashAI creates your own referral code the first time you open the invite screen, with a flashai.academy/r/… link that records visits as described above. You can see how many people entered your code, visits, revenue, commission and payment history on the partner page on the web. If you want to receive commission, you enter your PayPal email on the partner page; FlashAI only uses it to send commission by PayPal and keeps it with the payment history. When you delete your account, the name, email and PayPal email in your partner profile are deleted; commission and payment records that already exist are kept for reconciliation.

4d. Pronunciation trial before you have an account

In the trial when you first install the app, if you agree, a short recording of your voice reading the word (up to 6 seconds) is sent, together with that word, through FlashAI’s server to Microsoft Azure AI Speech to be scored; FlashAI does not store the recording. To limit the number of scored attempts, the server stores a random ID of this app install and a salted hash of your IP address (FlashAI does not store the IP address) for about 3 days.

5. How we use information

  • Authenticating you, signing you in and protecting your account.
  • Showing your profile information in the app.
  • Storing and syncing your study data for the features you use.
  • Providing AI features when you request them.
  • Managing access to free or paid plans.
  • Maintaining, securing and improving FlashAI.
  • Sending study reminders if you turn on notifications.
  • Measuring basic feature activity and handling feedback.
  • Recording referral codes, giving AI Credits, calculating commissions and paying them to referral partners by PayPal.

6. Storage and processing services

FlashAI uses Supabase for authentication and data storage; OpenAI for AI features; Microsoft Azure AI Speech to score pronunciation; Apple, Google or Facebook for social sign-in; RevenueCat with the App Store or Google Play to manage purchases and plan access; PayPal to send commission to referral partners; and Cloudflare to run referral links and the partner web page. These providers process data under their own policies.

Recipients of data must provide a level of data protection equivalent to FlashAI’s commitments in this policy, including security, purpose limitation and access control. We use OpenAI’s API services under the provider’s data processing terms, and we do not allow user data to be used for advertising.

FlashAI does not sell your personal information to third parties.

7. Retention and security

We apply appropriate measures, within the app’s capabilities, to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure, so please also protect your sign-in details and your device.

AI data is sent over HTTPS. Under OpenAI’s API policy, abuse monitoring logs may contain request content and are kept for up to 30 days by default, or longer when required by law or for safety. Actual retention depends on the type of data, the service and OpenAI’s policies. Withdrawing permission or deleting your FlashAI account does not immediately delete data that OpenAI has already received. See https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data and https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/.

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain your account and meet legal obligations. After you delete your account, your profile data, decks, flashcards, study progress and personal files are deleted; payment providers may keep some transaction records under their own obligations.

8. Your rights and requests

You can view and delete your account in the app at Profile → Delete Account. Deleting your account is permanent and deletes the personal data and study data linked to it. For accounts that use Sign in with Apple, the app verifies you again and revokes Sign in with Apple access when you delete the account.

Deleting your FlashAI account does not cancel a subscription made through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it separately in the store’s subscription settings to avoid renewal. You can also contact hello.truonghuy@gmail.com about data requests.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, services or related regulations change. The new version will be posted on this page, with the update date at the top.
